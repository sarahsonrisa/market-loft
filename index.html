Common Questions

What you need to know.

What is a fractional CMO?

A fractional CMO is a senior marketing executive who works with a company on a part-time or contract basis. They provide the same strategic leadership as a full-time CMO — go-to-market strategy, team direction, demand generation, positioning — at a fraction of the cost. Ideal for startups and scale-ups that need senior expertise without a full-time headcount.

How is MarketLoft different from other fractional CMO services?

MarketLoft brings rare depth in construction technology marketing with 15+ years combined experience. We cap our roster to 10 active clients to protect quality. Every engagement ends with documented processes your next hire can use from day one — that's an investment in your team, not just a consulting engagement.

What industries does MarketLoft specialize in?

MarketLoft specializes in construction technology and proptech — including preconstruction software, construction management platforms, and construction tech SaaS. We also serve early-stage B2B SaaS companies. Our construction tech expertise spans 15+ years across Autodesk, Pype, and ConCntric.

How much does a fractional CMO cost?

MarketLoft offers three retainer tiers: Steady ($3,000–$5,000/month, 10–15 hours), Momentum ($6,000–$10,000/month, 20–30 hours), and Full Send (custom pricing, 40+ hours — includes the full fractional team). Fixed-price packages and standalone deliverables are also available. A full-time CMO typically costs $250,000–$400,000 annually including benefits.

What does a fractional CMO engagement look like day-to-day?

Depending on the tier, engagements include strategy calls, standup attendance, direct ownership of deliverables, vendor and agency direction, and executive alignment. We operate as a genuine member of your leadership team — not an outside consultant who shows up monthly with a report.

Do you work with companies outside of construction tech?

Yes. While construction tech and proptech are our primary verticals, we work with early-stage B2B SaaS startups and scale-ups across industries. Our go-to-market frameworks, ICP development, ABM programs, and content strategy capabilities apply broadly to B2B companies at the seed through Series B stage.
